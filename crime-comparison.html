How to read this

These agencies are not perfectly comparable

Long Beach and Los Angeles counts come from official open incident files, but they are different departments, with different coding, late-report rules, and street grids. A record is an offense row, not always a unique event. Addresses are 100-block anonymized; distances use Census-geocoded block points. Sexual-assault addresses are redacted in Long Beach and cannot be placed in a radius. El Dorado County Sheriff address-level data for 1117 Venezia Dr could not be retrieved, so that home is ranked on neighborhood estimates only. Do not treat this as a victimization forecast or an appraisal of safety.

Windows match the Carfax crime work: prior year is date_reported (or LAPD date_rptd) Nov 1, 2024 through Nov 1, 2025; latest year is Nov 1, 2025 through Nov 1, 2026. Both endpoints are inclusive, so Nov 1, 2025 appears in each window. That overlap did not change the Long Beach radius counts (zero locatable records that day in these radii). Categories are UCR-style violent (murder and nonnegligent manslaughter, rape, robbery, aggravated assault), burglary, vehicle break-in / auto theft (motor-vehicle theft, auto burglary, theft from a vehicle, and theft of vehicle parts), other larceny, and other (including simple assault, intimidation, vandalism, fraud, and drugs). DataLB for Long Beach was queried Nov 22, 2026 (data processed 8:00 a.m. PT). LAPD NIBRS data in the Frances radius runs through Oct 31, 2026. Long Beach open data is published with a 21-day delay.

Ranking, safest to least safe

  1. 1117 Venezia Dr, El Dorado Hills. Safest on the evidence that exists: CrimeGrade (updated November 2026) grades El Dorado Hills A+ overall, A+ for violent crime, and A for property crime, about 13 reported crimes a year per 1,000 residents. Official 0.25- and 0.5-mile incident counts could not be retrieved, so this rank is less certain than the three below.
  2. 2001 Carfax Ave, Long Beach. Lowest official radius counts of the three homes with incident data. The 2000 block of Carfax had zero records in both windows. Latest year: 24 locatable records within 0.25 mile (4 UCR-style violent) and 70 within 0.5 mile (6 UCR-style violent). CrimeGrade for Los Altos: overall B, violent A-, property C-.
  3. 2658 Knoxville Ave, Long Beach. Same city, same dataset, and same method as Carfax, but higher official counts in both radii, especially at 0.5 mile (122 latest-year records vs 70 at Carfax), with more residential burglary and more activity toward Willow and Spring. The 2600 block of Knoxville had two latest-year records (petty theft and credit-card fraud, both July 6, 2026). CrimeGrade for The Plaza is slightly better than Los Altos (overall B+, violent A, property C+), which does not override the official nearby counts.
  4. 3827 Frances Ave, Mar Vista. Far higher official LAPD counts: 138 latest-year records within 0.25 mile (13 UCR-style violent) and 344 within 0.5 mile (31 UCR-style violent). Vehicle crime and other property crime dominate, and Venice Blvd sits inside the quarter-mile. CrimeGrade for Mar Vista: overall B-, violent A-, property D. The 3800 block of Frances had one latest-year petty theft (Aug 29, 2026).

This ranking leans on official radius counts where they exist, then on neighborhood grades, and it treats Venezia’s first-place finish as provisional until address-level Sheriff data can be checked.

What stands out at each address

  • Carfax: the block itself was quiet both years. Nearby risk is vehicle and vehicle-parts crime, plus a small number of aggravated assaults toward Stearns and Palo Verde. Broad violent records inside 0.5 mile fell year over year; UCR-style violent records rose from 3 to 6 (small counts). Three sex-offense records in RD 634 in the latest year have redacted addresses and could not be placed.
  • Knoxville: official counts are higher than Carfax at both radii, and the 0.5-mile circle reaches Willow and Spring. Residential burglary was more common here in the prior year (7 records inside 0.25 mile, several on the 2800 block of Iroquois, which also recurs in the latest year). Latest-year UCR-style violent inside 0.25 mile was one aggravated assault at 2700 Josie on Sep 19, 2026 (0.08 mi). Some sex-offense records in nearby reporting districts were redacted and excluded from radius totals.
  • Frances: counts are much higher in both years and both radii, with vehicle break-ins and thefts the largest slices and Venice Blvd inside 0.25 mile. Same-block activity was still light (one petty theft in the latest year). Year over year, 0.25-mile totals eased slightly (145 to 138) while 0.5-mile totals rose (318 to 344), led by theft and vehicle records. These figures should not be added to Long Beach counts as if they were one system.
  • Venezia: no official same-block, 0.25-mile, or 0.5-mile counts are in this Page. CrimeMapping.com requests were blocked from this environment. CA DOJ OpenJustice Crimes and Clearances files could not be downloaded (the portal returned a login or HTML page, not a CSV). The Sheriff’s citizen incident feed at https://eldoradosheriff.citizenrims.com/ exists but a complete two-year, radius-filtered extract was not obtained. The only quantitative source used here is CrimeGrade for El Dorado Hills as a whole, which is a neighborhood estimate, not this street.
